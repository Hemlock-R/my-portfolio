Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how I handle information that you choose to provide through my portfolio website. I aim to keep the information I collect limited to what is needed to respond to your requests.

Information You Provide

When you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and message. You choose whether to submit this information when contacting me.

How I Use Your Information

I use information submitted through the contact form to read and respond to your message, discuss a project or service, and communicate with you about your request.

How Your Information Is Stored

Contact form submissions are stored in the website's database so that I can manage and respond to messages. I take reasonable steps to protect this information from unauthorized access.

Sharing Your Information

I do not use the information submitted through the contact form for unrelated purposes. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the website, respond to a request, or meet a legal requirement.

How Long Information Is Kept

Contact information may be kept for as long as it is reasonably needed to manage communication and project-related requests. Information that is no longer needed may be removed.

Your Questions

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or about information you have submitted through the website, please use the contact information provided on the portfolio.
